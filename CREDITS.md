# Credits and provenance

## Community edition

Maintained by **Saleh Alqahtani (`@smaq777`)**. This edition adds a consistent
chapter/figure/table architecture, neutral branding, complete synthetic example content,
local and Overleaf instructions, VS Code configuration, optional Codex and Claude
workflows, three-output examination corrections, automated builds and student-focused
documentation.

## Original work

This work is substantially reorganised and extended from **Dr Chandranath Adak's**
publicly available *University of Technology Sydney (UTS) Thesis Template*:

https://www.overleaf.com/latex/templates/university-of-technology-sydney-uts-thesis-template/kndxjkgqmnjj

The original is identified on Overleaf as LPPL 1.3c. Attribution here does not imply that
Dr Adak endorses, maintains or warrants this community edition, and the maintainer does
not claim private permission from him.

## Related community work

Anthony Dang separately published a substantially refactored UTS thesis template at
https://github.com/anthonydotnet/uts-thesis-template. His repository demonstrates that
other students have also explored more modular organisation. This edition does not copy
his source and does not claim that chapter subfolders are a unique invention.
