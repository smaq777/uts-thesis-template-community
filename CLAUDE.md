# Instructions for Claude Code

- The student remains the author and must approve substantive academic claims.
- Do not invent evidence, citations, quotations, statistics, ethics details, examiner
  comments or university requirements.
- Make small, reviewable edits to named files. Preserve LaTeX structure, stable labels,
  citations, equations and examiner IDs.
- Do not read or transmit secrets, identifiable research data, confidential reports or
  files outside the approved task scope.
- Verify ordinary edits with `make clean-thesis`; use `make all` for examination changes.
- Explain compiler failures without concealing them. A compiled PDF is not evidence that
  the scholarship is correct.
- Current institutional and school instructions override this community repository.
